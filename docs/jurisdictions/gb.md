# United Kingdom (legislation.gov.uk) - `GB`, `GB-SCT`, `GB-WLS`

Status: **every Act and Measure with a revised text that is not marked repealed in full** (3,940 works), profile
`draft_unverified`. Current revised text of each; the full point-in-time history of nine works (the original sample).
Supreme Court judgments are in [gb-case-law.md](gb-case-law.md); the parser, dates and the 159-version history run of the
sample are in [test-results.md](../test-results.md) and [temporal-model.md](../temporal-model.md).

## Licence and fair use (read 2026-10-15)

- Everything is under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)
  (site footer); attribution "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0."
- [Fair use policy](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/fair-use-policy): identify the bot (done); follow
  [robots.txt](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/robots.txt) (`Crawl-delay: 5`; only `*/data.pdf`, `*/data.docx`, `/defralex`
  disallowed; the connector reads it once per run, checks every URL against it and uses the larger delay); at most 1,500
  requests per 5 minutes (ULIS: 60). The policy says "a large, one-off crawl" should use "a different way to download
  content"; the developer pages (formats, searching, URIs) offer no bulk download, only the Atom listings and the New
  Legislation and Publication Log feeds, so the listings plus one `data.akn` per Act are the documented route. It asks to be
  contacted only if its rules would prevent the intended use or the activity may affect performance; neither applies at 1
  request per 5 s. The connector stops on 401/403/451/429, never works around a refusal, records a missing work and goes on,
  and gives up after five failures in a row.

## What is in the manifest

The site's listings (`/<type>/data.feed?results-count=1000`, 43 pages of up to 1,000) give each item's title and the link to
its latest version: a dated link means a revised text exists, `.../enacted` means only the text as enacted. A work is listed
when it has a revised text and its title has no "(repealed ...)" label (the editors' label for an Act repealed in full).

| Type | Items on the site | Listed |
|---|---|---|
| `ukpga` UK Public General Acts | 17,560 (13,253 only as enacted, 863 repealed) | 3,444 |
| `ukla` UK Local Acts | 20,171 (20,154 only as enacted) | 16 |
| `asp` Acts of the Scottish Parliament | 402 | 385 |
| `anaw` / `asc` Acts of the Assembly / Senedd Cymru | 44 / 34 | 42 / 33 |
| `mwa` Measures of the Assembly | 22 | 19 |
| `uksi` statutory instruments | 109,369 | 1 (sample) |

**Statutory instruments are left out**: at least 6,872 UK SIs have a revised text and no "(revoked" label (counted in pages
1-100 of 110, years 2026-1968; the rest add a handful), above the ~5,000 asked for, and the parent-Act test cannot be made
from the listing. Not ingested either: Acts of the Northern Ireland Assembly (`nia`, one flag away in the builder), Church
Measures, Acts of earlier parliaments. Acts before 1963 are identified by regnal-year path (`ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58`); their
work URI is `/gb/ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58` because "1859 c. 1" names two different Acts (Burial Act and Clerk of the Council Act).
Cross-references written in calendar form (`ukpga/1945/18`) therefore stay unresolved for those Acts.

## Scale (extrapolated from the slice below)

3,940 works = 3,931 current-only + 9 with history: about **4,125 requests (4,081 documents, 43 listing pages, robots.txt),
5.7 hours at the 5 s crawl delay**, about 1.5 GB of raw responses (mean 343 KB, median 52 KB, largest 4 MB), about **1.8 million
provision versions, 1.6 million passages, 4.5 GB of database** (2.3 KB per provision version with its passage and indexes;
469 provision versions and 1.1 MB per Act on average). Ingest takes about 1 s per Act from the cache.

**Run it in batches or against a database with current statistics.** A run is one transaction. On a database that autovacuum
had vacuumed while the transaction was open (after a rolled-back run, or just because the tables start empty) the planner
believed the tables empty and took 2 minutes per Act. `Ingestor` now ANALYZEs its tables inside the transaction (at the
start, every 30 s, before the end) after taking their lock with NOWAIT, which also keeps autovacuum off them
(`tests/test_legislation_gov_uk_wide.py::test_a_run_keeps_autovacuum_off_its_tables`). For a source this size
`scripts/ingest_in_batches.py gb-legislation-gov-uk --batch 50` also commits as it goes.

## Slice run (not the whole thing)

`ulis_wuk`: 56 current-only works (every 70th of the manifest: 49 `ukpga`, 1 `ukla`, 5 `asp`, 1 `anaw`) plus the 9 history
works. 56 Acts: 26,267 provision versions, 22,967 passages, 64 citation links, **0 errors, 0 conflicts**, 54 s from the
cache; 9 history works: 159 versions, 38,851 provision versions created, 301,080 corroborated, 64 restated, 35,370 passages,
0 errors, 172 s. `verify-sources`: **215 pass, 0 fail**, 366,198 units. Searches (`GB-ENG`/`GB-SCT`, 2026-10-15):
"supply of a psychoactive substance offence" -> `Psychoactive Substances Act 2016, s 5(2)`; "hijacking of aircraft offence" ->
`Aviation Security Act 1982, s 1(1)`; "fireworks licence offence" (GB-SCT) -> `Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Articles (Scotland) Act
2022, s 48(1)`; all with `REVISED_TEXT`, `SOURCE_NOT_AUTHENTIC`, `PROFILE_UNVERIFIED`, `AS_OF_AFTER_CURRENCY`.

Defects found at this scale and fixed (regression tests in the wide test file): (1) the connector rejected Acts before 1963
(regnal paths; sessions can span three years: `Geo6/12-13-14/103`); (2) a deed in a Schedule (Chevening Estate Act 1959)
repeats paragraph numbers, so two provisions had the same eId and dates and the database refused the second as a version
ending the day it starts - the run aborted at its end and rolled back everything; the parser now suffixes repeated eIds
(`schedule-paragraph-1-dup2`) and the pipeline reports a failed validity derivation instead of aborting; (3) a document
without provisions is `ParseSkipped`, not an error.

## Known gaps

- Statutory instruments, `nia`, Church Measures, Acts held only as enacted (mostly repealed before 1991, which the site does not
  revise); Welsh-language texts; Acts marked repealed in full are skipped on the label, not checked against their text.
- History only for nine works; for every other Act earlier dates are reported as history not indexed. The site's revised data
  starts on 1991-02-01 for older Acts.
- Everything else in the original notes: outstanding effects are reported, not applied; revised texts are not authentic.
