# Poland (PL) - Sejm ELI API (Dziennik Ustaw)

**Status:** profile `draft_unverified` (not reviewed by counsel). Source `pl-eli-sejm`, connector `eli_sejm`, parser
`eli_sejm_bundle`, module `pl`. Language `pl` (no PostgreSQL text-search configuration for Polish exists, so the `simple`
configuration is used: no stemming, inflected forms match only when identical). Widened from six codes to every ustawa
with a current consolidated text (789 instruments).

## Source and licence

- The ELI API of the Chancellery of the Sejm, <https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli>, serves records and texts of the Dziennik Ustaw.
  Documentation: [eli.html](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli.html), [eli_pl.html](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli_pl.html) (last
  updated 2025-11-25), [OpenAPI](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/openapi/): **no terms of use, licence, rate limit or ban on
  automated use**. [api.sejm.gov.pl/robots.txt](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/robots.txt) is HTTP 404 (no restrictions, re-checked
  2026-10-15); [eli.gov.pl/robots.txt](https://eli.gov.pl/robots.txt) is `User-Agent: *` / `Disallow:`. `isap.sejm.gov.pl`
  answered 403 to the bot in an earlier probe and is **not used**.
- Basis for reuse: Prawo autorskie art. 4 pts 1-2, read from the API's own text
  ([art. 4](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2022/2509/text.html/artykul%3D4)): "Nie stanowią przedmiotu prawa autorskiego:
  1) akty normatywne lub ich urzędowe projekty; 2) urzędowe dokumenty, materiały, znaki i symbole". AMBER in
  `docs/licence-review.md` (statutory basis only; database rights in the compilation not examined).
- Status of the text. The Dziennik Ustaw publishes the act as announced (`ogl`) and, as an *obwieszczenie* of the Marszałek
  Sejmu, its consolidated text (*tekst jednolity*, `tj`; [art. 16](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2019/1461/text.html/artykul%3D16)
  of the Act of 20 July 2000: at least every 12 months if the act was amended). A "tekst ujednolicony" (`uj`) is "bez mocy
  prawnej" and PDF only: not ingested. ULIS ingests the **tekst jednolity**: `text_origin = official_consolidation`,
  `authoritative = false`, authority `official_non_authentic` (the authentic law is the act and its amendments).

## What is ingested (manifest `config/manifests/pl-eli-sejm.yaml`, generated by `scripts/build_eli_manifest.py`)

The API's own search lists every announcement of a consolidated text of an ustawa (`GET /acts/search?publisher=DU&type=Obwieszczenie&title=jednolitego+tekstu+ustawy`:
5 079 records, 11 requests of 500). An act is listed when one of its announcements is in force today (812 acts); its text is
its newest announcement for which the API offers HTML (PDF is never parsed). **789 instruments**: 409 with their current text,
380 with an older one because the current text (announced 2025-2026: 483 announcements, none with HTML) is PDF only, and
23 acts in force with no HTML text at all are left out. The six pilot codes keep two successive texts each (11 texts); the
other 783 acts one text. Not listed: acts in force without a consolidated text, regulations, Monitor Polski, treaties.
**The Constitution (`DU/1997/483`) is PDF only (`textHTML: false`, checked 2026-10-15): not ingested.**

## Structure, dates and versions

- Work URI `/pl/du/<year>/<position>`, expression `.../pol@<legal-status date>`. Provisions: article (`art-415`; `art-22_1` =
  22¹), § (`-par1`), ust. (`-ust1`), pkt (`-pkt2`), lit. (`-lita`), `preamble`, `division` (a heading that only says "(... -
  uchylony)"). Księga ... Oddział, and "A."-type divisions the source labels without naming, are context headings.
- Wider markup than the six codes use: "- text" lists stay lines of their letter; a table is lines (rows, cells separated
  by " | "); a formula is the TeX the source gives (`\( ... \)`); a second heading line of a division belongs to the heading.
- Each text is a whole-act snapshot (`revision_snapshot`, `SNAPSHOT_DATES`; `currency_date` = legal-status date). Each act's
  API record is a change report on its newest text: later amending acts and later (PDF-only) consolidated texts are
  outstanding changes without text. Footnotes are notes; a unit that is only "(uchylony)" is `repealed`. The
  licence review's date concern (an earlier text runs to the next text's date even if an amending act took effect between)
  applies to the six pilot acts with two texts only; the 783 others have one text, so no end date is inferred for them.
- A manifest option `on_instrument_error: skip` (committed) reports an act that fails as a run error and goes on; a refusal stops.

## Citations (module `pl`) and profile

`art. 415 k.c.`, `art. 148 § 2 pkt 1 k.k.`, `art. 22¹ k.c.`; an act without an abbreviation by the long form of its title:
`art. 141m ust. 3 ustawy z dnia 29 sierpnia 1997 r. - Prawo bankowe` (new: `generic_cite_as`, first word in the genitive, rest as
printed). Profile: civil law, unitary, treaty reception `monist` (to verify); no formal precedent. No EU membership added.

## Scale and results (2026-10-15)

- **Full manifest:** 789 instruments, 2 377 requests (three per act), 3.3 hours at the 5 s delay, about 0.55 GB downloaded
  (mean text 680 KB). Estimated database: about 375 000 provision versions, about 0.9 GB (2.3 KB each, from the slice).
- **Slice** (6 pilot + every 19th of the others = 46 instruments, 97 documents, `ulis_wpl`): first run 11 of 46 acts failed on
  markup (tables, lists, formulas, unnamed divisions); after the parser fixes 0 errors. Ingest: 97 documents, 51 texts,
  27 989 provision versions + 7 440 corroborated, 26 429 passages. `verify-sources`: 97 of 97 pass (35 429 units). Database 92 MB
  (10 MB empty), 1.6 MB per text.
- Searches: "Kto z winy swej wyrządził drugiemu szkodę" -> art. 415 k.c.; "pracodawca może rozwiązać umowę o pracę bez
  wypowiedzenia" -> art. 52 § 1 k.p.; "Plan naprawy obejmuje czynności służące utrzymaniu ..." -> art. 141m ust. 3 ustawy z dnia 29
  sierpnia 1997 r. - Prawo bankowe; "wskaźnik zwiększający dla szkół danego typu" -> art. 14 ust. 2 ustawy z dnia 27 października
  2017 r. o finansowaniu zadań oświatowych. Failure: "odszkodowanie za szkodę wyrządzoną czynem niedozwolonym" (`--as-of
  2024-01-15`) returned art. 819 § 3 k.c., not art. 415 (no stemming).
- Tests: `tests/test_eli_sejm.py` 31 (5 new: wider markup from a real fixture, generic citation, skip mode, refusal stops a skip-mode
  run, manifest loads). Full suite 1 294 passed against a scratch test database; ruff, ruff format and mypy clean.

## Known gaps

380 acts show a text older than the current one (PDF only since 2025); 23 acts and the Constitution have none; no acts without a
consolidated text, regulations, Monitor Polski, treaties or case law; no per-provision dates; the announcement text, footnotes on
division headings and cross-reference links are not kept; unknown markup stops the parse of that act; re-ingesting a recorded
text from changed bytes is refused (the change reports are what refreshes).
