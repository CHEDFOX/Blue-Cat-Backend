# Latvia (LV) - Likumi.lv

**Status:** profile `draft_unverified` (not reviewed by counsel). Source `lv-likumi`, connector `likumi_lv`, parser
`likumi_lv_html`, module `lv`. Language `lv` (no PostgreSQL text-search configuration for Latvian exists, so the `simple`
configuration is used: no stemming, inflected forms match only when identical). Widened from ten documents to the laws and
Cabinet regulations in force (about 4 000 documents expected).

## What is ingested (manifest `config/manifests/lv-likumi.yaml`, generated by `scripts/build_likumi_manifest.py`)

The portal has no list of the acts in force: its listings are filled by a POST to the disallowed `/search.php`. The one list it
publishes is the sitemap named in robots.txt (4 requests, 30 MB, 125 436 addresses `/ta/id/<id>-<slug>`). A sitemap entry gives no
type or status, so the manifest lists **20 344 candidates**: the ten pilot documents, five curated "Par ..." laws, and every other
Latvian-language page except amending acts ("Grozījumi ..."), orders ("Par ..."), municipal rules, notices, minutes, treaties and
translations. The parser keeps a page whose `Veids` is a law (`likums`) or a Cabinet regulation (`noteikumi` of the Ministru
kabinets) and whose `Statuss` is "Spēkā esošs", and **skips** the rest (`ParseSkipped`, not an error). Sample of 160 random candidates:
32 ingested (27 regulations, 5 laws), 53 laws or regulations no longer in force, 75 other documents, 0 failures.

## Structure, dates and versions

- Work URI `/lv/likumi/<id>`, expression `.../lav@<edition start>`. Law units: article (`art-117`), part (daļa), point, sub-point,
  transitional provision (`pn-11`), preamble, EU reference. A **Cabinet regulation** (instrument type `regulation`, `number` = Nr.,
  title "Ministru kabineta 2011. gada 17. maija noteikumi Nr. 377 "..."") is numbered by points: `art-12`, sub-points `art-12-p12-1`
  (12.1.), letters, and its enabling provision ("Izdoti saskaņā ar ...", `legal-basis`). Headings (nodaļa, I., 1.) are context only.
- Whole-act snapshot (`revision_snapshot`): every provision runs from the edition's start; where the page states the edition's end,
  `valid_to` is the day after; editions for the future are outstanding changes without text. A page with a single edition has an empty
  edition list and names its start in `version_date`. No history (earlier editions are disallowed): earlier dates are
  `history_not_indexed`. The page states no "up to date" date (`CURRENCY_UNKNOWN`).
- **Annexes (pielikumi) and tables are not ingested**; a document that has them says so in `ext.omitted`. Amendment and deletion
  annotations are notes; a unit that is only "(Izslēgts ...)" is `repealed`.

## Scale and results (2026-10-15)

- **Full manifest:** 20 344 requests = 28 hours at the 5 s delay (mean page 120 KB, ingested pages 190 KB: about 3 GB), about 4 000
  documents, about 360 000 provisions, database about 1.2 GB (3.1 KB per provision version, from the slice). Skipped pages leave their
  raw copy in the object store (about 1.6 GB unreferenced).
- **Slice** (10 pilot + the 160 random candidates, `ulis_wlv`): 170 documents seen, 42 ingested, 128 skipped, 0 errors; 10 823 provision
  versions, 9 691 passages, 997 citation links (808 resolved). `verify-sources`: 42 of 42 pass. Database 42 MB (10 MB empty).
- Searches: "ietekmes uz vidi novērtējums" -> Ministru kabineta 2022. gada 18. oktobra noteikumu Nr. 653 4. punkts; "darba devējs
  uzteikt darba līgumu" -> Darba likuma 110. panta trešā daļa. Failures (no stemming): "vaislinieku sertifikācija", "atbalsta
  pieteikums apstiprināšana kārtība" and "Krimināllikums slepkavība" returned nothing.
- Tests: `tests/test_likumi_lv.py` 31 (9 new: Cabinet regulations from two real pages, annexes disclosed, pages skipped, genitives,
  robots ids, skip mode, refusal, manifest). Full suite 1 294 passed against a scratch test database; ruff, ruff format, mypy clean.

## Known gaps

Current edition only; amending acts, orders, municipal rules, case law and the regulators' rules are not ingested; "Par ..." laws are
candidates only if named; annexes, tables, the enacting formula, signatures, English translations not ingested; Cabinet-regulation
numbering other than points and letters is text; the page can change between the manifest and the run (the sitemap is the one
list); a page with an unrecognised layout (for example the unstructured `neko-body` pages seen on some old amending acts) fails and is reported.
